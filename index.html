LEED Gold certification, FSC-certified timber

The natural warmth of wood, with a smaller carbon footprint.

Mass timber gives The Weave exposed wood ceilings and columns, so the office feels calm, warm and close to nature. The building is designed to achieve LEED Gold certification, an easy fit for your company's sustainability goals.

MERV 13 filtration, CO2 monitoring, operable windows

Daylight, fresh air and terraces, so afternoons don't drag.

Skylights and slim floor plates bring daylight deep into every floor. Windows open, and the air is refreshed 30% more than code requires. When a meeting needs a change of scene, take it to a terrace or the open-air stairs.

Flexible floors that grow with your team.

Open, flexible floors make it easy to move between team work, focus time and private meetings. As your team grows or changes, the space adapts with it. Pre-leasing now means you plan the buildout from day one.
